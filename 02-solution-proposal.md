# 02. Solution Proposal

## 1. Solution Selection Approach

Following the business and organizational analysis, the project team explored potential digital solutions that could address the limitations in practical shrimp farming training while supporting VNUA's broader digital transformation objectives.

The proposed solution needed to meet several key requirements:

- Improve practical learning experiences for aquaculture students.
- Provide a safe environment for students to practice decision-making.
- Reduce the risks and costs associated with real-world experimentation.
- Be feasible for large-scale implementation.
- Support the long-term collection and utilization of learning data.

Based on these requirements, the team considered two primary technology options: a **shrimp farming simulation game platform** and a **Virtual Reality (VR) application**.

## 2. Alternative Solutions Considered

### Option 1: Shrimp Farming Simulation Game Platform

The first option was a digital game platform designed to simulate the shrimp farming process.

The platform would provide a visual and interactive learning environment where students could experience different stages of shrimp farming, make decisions, receive simulation feedback, and accumulate practical experience.

The proposed solution could also support knowledge standardization and the integration of automated performance assessment.

However, developing an educational simulation game requires a significant initial investment and collaboration with domain experts to develop realistic scenarios and learning content.

### Option 2: Virtual Reality (VR) Application

The second option was a Virtual Reality application that would allow users to observe and interact with a 3D shrimp farming environment.

The main advantage of VR is its high level of immersion and realism. Students could experience a simulated farming environment and practice in a safe setting.

However, the solution would require specialized hardware and higher technology investment. These requirements could limit the feasibility of large-scale deployment across students.

### Solution Comparison

| Criteria | Simulation Game Platform | VR Application |
|---|---|---|
| Practical learning | High | High |
| Accessibility | High | Medium |
| Scalability | High | Low to Medium |
| Hardware requirements | Relatively low | High |
| Initial implementation cost | High | High |
| Suitability for large-scale deployment | High | Lower |
| Automated performance assessment | Supported | Potentially supported |
| Data collection potential | High | High |

## 3. Selected Solution: ShrimpSim

After evaluating the two alternatives, the project team selected the **Shrimp Farming Simulation Game Platform**, named **ShrimpSim**, as the proposed digital transformation solution for VNUA.

The simulation game approach was selected because it addressed the immediate need for more practical shrimp farming training while offering greater feasibility for large-scale deployment compared with the VR alternative.

ShrimpSim was designed as a digital platform that enables aquaculture students to simulate a complete shrimp farming process. Users take on the role of a shrimp farmer, make decisions throughout different stages of the farming process, receive simulated feedback, and learn from the outcomes of their decisions.

The solution was expected to support practical learning while reducing the risks and costs associated with real-world experimentation.
