# 06. Expected Impact and Project Performance Control

## 1. Expected Project Impact

ShrimpSim was proposed as a digital transformation project for Vietnam National University of Agriculture (VNUA), with the aim of applying simulation technology to support practical learning in aquaculture education.

As the project was developed as an academic proposal, the impacts presented in this section represent the expected outcomes and potential contributions of the proposed solution rather than actual results after implementation.

The proposed ShrimpSim platform was expected to create value in several key areas.

### 1.1. Educational Impact

ShrimpSim was expected to provide students with a more interactive environment for practicing and applying aquaculture knowledge.

Through simulation scenarios, students could experience different farming situations, make decisions, and observe the potential outcomes of those decisions without depending entirely on physical farming environments.

The proposed solution was therefore expected to support:

* Practical application of theoretical knowledge.
* Increased interaction during the learning process.
* Opportunities for students to experience different farming scenarios.
* Reduced dependence on physical environments for some practical learning activities.

### 1.2. Operational Impact

The proposed platform was also expected to provide VNUA with an additional digital tool to support teaching and learning activities.

Lecturers could potentially use the platform as a supporting environment for organizing learning activities and evaluating student performance throughout simulation scenarios.

The project was expected to contribute to a more technology-enabled approach to practical education.

### 1.3. Data and Long-Term Development Impact

In addition to its educational function, ShrimpSim was designed to generate data from user interactions, decisions, and simulation activities.

If implemented, the platform could gradually accumulate data that may support:

* Analysis of user behavior and learning activities.
* Evaluation of simulation outcomes.
* Research and data analysis.
* Future development of data-driven applications.

This data could become a long-term asset supporting the continued development of the ShrimpSim ecosystem.

### Expected Impact Overview

```text
ShrimpSim
    │
    ├── Educational Impact
    │      └── Support practical and interactive learning
    │
    ├── Operational Impact
    │      └── Support technology-enabled teaching activities
    │
    └── Data and Long-Term Development Impact
           └── Generate and accumulate data for future development
```

## 2. Proposed KPIs for Project Performance Control

To monitor and control the effectiveness of the ShrimpSim project after implementation, the project team proposed a set of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

The proposed KPIs were developed based on the project's objectives and relevant benchmarks and reference information considered during the project analysis.

These indicators were intended to provide a framework for monitoring whether the implemented project performs in line with its intended objectives. They do not represent actual results achieved by ShrimpSim.

The proposed KPI framework covers five key areas:

1. **Annual Revenue Growth** – to monitor the project's financial contribution.
2. **Student Participation Rate** – to monitor the level of student engagement with the platform.
3. **Academic Achievement After Using the Game** – to evaluate the potential effectiveness of the platform in supporting learning outcomes.
4. **Number of Students Using the Platform** – to monitor the adoption and usage of ShrimpSim.
5. **Student Retention in the Major** – to monitor whether the proposed learning approach may contribute to students continuing their studies in the aquaculture major.

The five KPIs were proposed as indicators for continuous monitoring and evaluation if the project were implemented. Each KPI should be assessed against its corresponding target or benchmark established during the project planning process.

Overall, the proposed project was expected to contribute to the digital transformation of aquaculture education at VNUA by combining simulation-based learning with long-term data development.

### 2.1. Annual Revenue Growth

**Purpose**

Annual Revenue Growth was proposed as a financial KPI to monitor the project's ability to generate increasing revenue over time.

The project team selected this indicator because revenue growth reflects the potential sustainability of the project by measuring changes in revenue across different periods. It can also be used to monitor the commercialization performance of the proposed solution, as well as its potential to expand and attract the market in the medium and long term.

**Measurement**

The KPI can be monitored by comparing the proposed revenue of each year with the previous year.

The projected revenue figures used as reference in the project are shown below:

| Year                           |   1 |     2 |     3 |     4 |     5 |     6 |     7 |
| ------------------------------ | --: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: |
| Proposed Revenue (Million VND) | 600 |   800 | 1,100 | 1,200 | 1,600 | 1,800 | 2,100 |
| Annual Revenue Growth          |  0% | 33.3% | 37.5% |  9.1% | 33.3% | 12.5% | 16.7% |

**Use in Project Performance Control**

If ShrimpSim were implemented, this KPI could be monitored annually to compare actual revenue growth against the financial projections established by the project team.

The purpose of this comparison would be to identify whether the project's financial performance is developing in line with the proposed financial plan and to provide a basis for reviewing the project's commercialization and growth strategy.

> **Note:** The revenue figures and growth rates above are projected values developed for the academic project's financial model. They are used as reference benchmarks for project performance control and do not represent actual revenue generated by ShrimpSim.

### 2.2. Student Participation Rate

**Purpose**

The Student Participation Rate was proposed to monitor the level of engagement of students in the aquaculture major with the ShrimpSim platform.

As the platform was specifically designed for students in the aquaculture major, this indicator can be used to evaluate whether the project is reaching its intended target users.

The participation rate can also provide information about the attractiveness of the platform and the suitability of the simulation content for the characteristics of the major.

**Measurement**

The Student Participation Rate can be measured by comparing the number of students who participate in or use the ShrimpSim platform with the total number of students in the target group.

```text
Student Participation Rate
=
Number of Participating Students
────────────────────────────── × 100%
Total Target Students
```

The target group and measurement period should be defined during project implementation.

**Use in Project Performance Control**

If ShrimpSim were implemented, this KPI could be monitored periodically to determine the extent to which the platform reaches students in the aquaculture major.

Changes in the participation rate could provide a basis for evaluating:

* The level of adoption among the target users.
* The attractiveness of the platform.
* The suitability of the simulation content for students in the major.

The results could then be used to review and adjust the project's implementation and user engagement approach when necessary.

> **Note:** This KPI was proposed as a performance monitoring indicator for the project. It does not represent an actual participation rate achieved by ShrimpSim.

### 2.3. Academic Achievement After Using the Game

**Purpose**

Academic Achievement After Using the Game was proposed to monitor the academic achievements or performance obtained by students after experiencing the ShrimpSim platform.

The project team proposed this KPI as an indicator for evaluating the indirect impact of the simulation game on educational quality.

By monitoring the achievements or academic performance of students after using the platform, the project could assess whether the simulation-based learning approach contributes to improving students' knowledge, skills, and professional capabilities.

**Measurement**

This KPI can be measured by tracking the academic achievements or performance outcomes of students after they have participated in learning activities using ShrimpSim.

The measurement criteria should be defined during implementation based on the academic evaluation approach applied by VNUA.

The results can then be compared across different periods or student groups to support the evaluation of the platform's potential contribution to learning outcomes.

**Use in Project Performance Control**

If ShrimpSim were implemented, this KPI could be used to monitor the educational effectiveness of the platform over time.

The results could provide a basis for evaluating whether the proposed simulation activities are supporting their intended educational objectives and whether adjustments to the simulation content or learning approach may be necessary.

> **Note:** This KPI was proposed as a project performance monitoring indicator. It does not represent actual academic achievements obtained through ShrimpSim, as the project was developed as an academic proposal.

### 2.4 Number of Students Using the Platform

**Purpose**

The Number of Students Using the Platform was proposed to monitor the actual scale of platform adoption.

This indicator reflects the number of students who access and use ShrimpSim and can provide an indication of the project's potential for growth.

An increase in the number of students using the platform would create a broader user base for evaluating the practical applicability of the proposed solution and supporting future product development strategies.

**Measurement**

The KPI can be measured by tracking the number of unique students who use the ShrimpSim platform during a defined measurement period.

The measurement period may be determined based on the implementation plan, such as by semester or academic year.

```text
Number of Students Using the Platform
=
Total unique students who accessed or used ShrimpSim
during the defined measurement period
```

**Use in Project Performance Control**

If ShrimpSim were implemented, this KPI could be monitored over time to track changes in the scale of platform usage.

The results could be used to evaluate:

* The actual scale of platform adoption.
* Changes in the number of users over time.
* The potential for expanding the use of the platform.
* The practical applicability of the proposed solution.

This information could support future decisions related to platform development and expansion.

> **Note:** This KPI was proposed as a performance monitoring indicator and does not represent the actual number of students who have used ShrimpSim.

### 2.5 Student Retention in the Major

**Purpose**

The Number of Students Continuing to Study in the Major was proposed to monitor the potential role of ShrimpSim in supporting student retention within the aquaculture major.

The project team selected this indicator because a simulation-based learning environment could potentially increase students' interest and engagement with their field of study.

Monitoring the number of students who continue studying in the major could therefore provide an additional perspective for evaluating the educational and career-orientation value of the proposed platform.

**Measurement**

The KPI can be measured by tracking the number or proportion of students who continue their studies in the aquaculture major over a defined period.

The specific measurement approach should be determined during implementation based on the available student data and the evaluation framework used by VNUA.

```text
Student Retention Rate
=
Number of Students Continuing in the Major
────────────────────────────────────────── × 100%
Total Number of Students in the Target Group
```

**Use in Project Performance Control**

If ShrimpSim were implemented, this KPI could be monitored over time to evaluate whether the proposed learning approach may contribute to maintaining students' interest in the major.

The indicator could provide information relevant to:

* Student retention within the major.
* Student interest and engagement with their field of study.
* The potential reduction of major changes or discontinuation of study.
* The educational and career-orientation value of the proposed solution.

The results could be used as one of the indicators for reviewing the long-term effectiveness of the project.

> **Note:** This KPI was proposed as a performance monitoring indicator. It does not represent an actual retention result achieved by ShrimpSim.

## 3. Expected Organizational Impact on VNUA

The project team assessed the potential organizational impact of ShrimpSim by comparing the existing situation identified during the project analysis with the expected changes if the proposed solution were implemented.

| Area                      | Before Implementation                                                                                                                                                                             | Expected Impact After Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Leadership**            | VNUA had a general vision for digital transformation but lacked practical projects that could demonstrate its effectiveness. The organizational culture emphasized solidarity and mutual support. | ShrimpSim could serve as an example of digital transformation in education, providing leadership with a practical basis for evaluating and promoting similar digital initiatives in other faculties.                                          |
| **Human Resources**       | VNUA had a large and qualified teaching workforce. However, lecturers mainly relied on theoretical teaching and had limited visual tools to support practical learning.                           | Students could experience more interactive simulation activities, while lecturers could have an additional teaching tool to support learning activities and monitor student performance.                                                      |
| **Risk Management**       | The project identified the need to reduce the risk of falling behind in technology and improve competitiveness. Physical training activities could also involve practical risks.                  | The proposed solution could help reduce certain physical risks while shifting risk management toward a more proactive approach to digital and technical risks, including the ownership and protection of intellectual property.               |
| **Technology**            | Modern facilities were available, but practical technology applications were mainly limited to administrative systems, websites, and email.                                                       | Technology could be applied more directly to teaching and learning activities. The platform could also provide a foundation for future AI and machine learning applications to analyze data and potentially personalize learning experiences. |
| **Data Strategy**         | The project did not identify an existing simulation-based data foundation related to the training process and shrimp farming activities.                                                          | ShrimpSim could gradually form an initial **data moat** based on training processes, shrimp farming activities, user behavior, and simulation data, creating a foundation for future analysis and research.                                   |
| **Innovation Capability** | There was an opportunity to further expand the application of technology in education and aquaculture.                                                                                            | ShrimpSim could promote research and innovation, particularly in the application of technology to education and aquaculture.                                                                                                                  |

> **Note:** The impacts presented above represent the project team's assessment of potential organizational changes if ShrimpSim were implemented. They do not represent actual post-implementation results.

## 4. Summary

ShrimpSim was proposed as a digital transformation project with the potential to support practical learning in aquaculture through a simulation-based platform.

The project team identified several expected areas of impact, including educational activities, teaching support, technology application, and long-term data development.

To support project performance control after implementation, five KPIs were proposed to monitor:

* Annual revenue growth.
* Student participation.
* Academic achievement after using the game.
* Number of students using the platform.
* Student retention in the major.

The project was also expected to create broader organizational impacts for VNUA across leadership, human resources, risk management, technology, data strategy, and innovation capability.

These impacts and KPIs represent the project team's proposed evaluation framework and expected outcomes if ShrimpSim were implemented. They do not represent actual post-implementation results.
