# 03. Project Planning

## 1. Project Scope

The ShrimpSim project was planned as a digital transformation initiative for Vietnam National University of Agriculture (VNUA), with a primary focus on improving practical training in shrimp farming through a simulation-based learning platform.

The proposed project scope included:

### In Scope

- Researching and defining user requirements for the shrimp farming simulation platform.
- Designing the core gameplay flow and shrimp farming scenarios.
- Developing a prototype of the ShrimpSim platform.
- Designing the user interface and learning experience.
- Integrating mechanisms for recording user decisions and simulation outcomes.
- Conducting Alpha testing with students and lecturers.
- Conducting Beta testing with selected real shrimp farming households.
- Collecting feedback and refining gameplay, scenarios, and user experience.
- Preparing the platform for official deployment and project handover.

### Out of Scope

The initial project scope focused on developing and validating the ShrimpSim platform. More advanced applications based on the generated data, such as large-scale predictive models and other future AI-driven capabilities, were considered long-term development opportunities rather than core deliverables of the initial implementation.

## 2. Project Delivery Approach

The ShrimpSim project was planned using an **Agile approach based on the Scrum framework**.

Scrum was selected because the project involved multiple stages of research, solution design, prototype development, testing, and refinement. The iterative approach allowed the project team to continuously collect feedback and adjust the product throughout the development process.

The project was divided into major implementation phases, while development activities within these phases could be managed through iterative cycles.

The general delivery approach was:

1. Define project requirements and research the shrimp farming process.
2. Design gameplay scenarios and the initial product concept.
3. Develop the prototype and core platform features.
4. Conduct testing and collect feedback from target users.
5. Refine the product based on testing results.
6. Prepare for official deployment and project handover.

### Scrum Application

Throughout the project, the team applied Scrum principles to manage tasks and monitor progress.

Key activities included:

- **Product Backlog:** Organizing and prioritizing project requirements, features, and development tasks.
- **Sprint Planning:** Selecting and planning the tasks to be completed during each development cycle.
- **Sprint Execution:** Designing, developing, testing, and refining the selected features.
- **Sprint Review:** Reviewing completed work and collecting feedback from relevant stakeholders.
- **Sprint Retrospective:** Evaluating the development process and identifying improvements for subsequent iterations.

## 3. Project Timeline & Key Phases

The proposed ShrimpSim project was planned over a period of approximately **10.5 months**, from initial research and planning to official deployment and project handover.

The implementation roadmap was divided into five major phases.

| Phase | Duration | Main Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| **Phase 1: Research & Planning** | 1 month | Research the shrimp farming process, identify user needs, define project requirements, and establish the initial project plan. |
| **Phase 2: Solution & Gameplay Design** | 2 months | Design the simulation concept, gameplay flow, farming scenarios, and initial user experience. |
| **Phase 3: Prototype Development & Alpha Testing** | 3 months | Develop the prototype and core features, followed by Alpha testing with students and lecturers. |
| **Phase 4: Beta Testing & Refinement** | 3 months | Conduct Beta testing with selected shrimp farming households, collect feedback, and refine the simulation experience. |
| **Phase 5: Deployment & Handover** | 1.5 months | Prepare the final version for deployment, complete project documentation, and hand over the platform. |

## 4. Key Activities and Deliverables

The project plan defined key activities and expected deliverables for each implementation phase.

| Phase | Key Activities | Expected Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Research & Planning** | Research the shrimp farming process; identify user needs; define project requirements; prepare the project plan. | User requirements, project scope, initial project plan, and research findings. |
| **Phase 2: Solution & Gameplay Design** | Design the simulation concept, gameplay flow, shrimp farming scenarios, and user experience. | Solution design, gameplay flow, simulation scenarios, and initial UI/UX design. |
| **Phase 3: Prototype Development & Alpha Testing** | Develop the prototype and core features; prepare testing activities; collect feedback from students and lecturers. | Functional prototype, Alpha testing results, user feedback, and improvement requirements. |
| **Phase 4: Beta Testing & Refinement** | Conduct testing with selected shrimp farming households; evaluate the realism and usability of the simulation; refine gameplay and scenarios. | Beta testing results, stakeholder feedback, refined simulation scenarios, and an improved product version. |
| **Phase 5: Deployment & Handover** | Prepare the final version; complete project documentation; support deployment and handover. | Final platform version, project documentation, deployment preparation, and handover materials. |

### Validation and Feedback Loop

A key part of the project plan was the use of testing and stakeholder feedback to refine the solution.

The validation process was planned in two stages:

1. **Alpha Testing:** The prototype would be tested with students and lecturers to evaluate the learning experience, usability, and overall suitability of the platform.

2. **Beta Testing:** The refined product would then be tested with selected shrimp farming households to collect feedback from users with practical experience in shrimp farming.

Feedback collected during both testing stages would be used to identify improvement opportunities and refine the platform before official deployment.

## 5. Project Resources

The ShrimpSim project required a combination of domain expertise, technology capabilities, project management, and testing resources.

### 5.1 Human Resources

The proposed project team required the following key roles:

| Role | Key Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Project Manager** | Manage the project plan, timeline, resources, stakeholders, and overall project progress. |
| **Business Analyst** | Gather and analyze requirements, identify user needs, define functional requirements, and coordinate between stakeholders and the development team. |
| **Aquaculture Experts** | Provide domain knowledge, validate shrimp farming processes, and support the development of realistic farming scenarios. |
| **Game / Product Designer** | Design the simulation concept, gameplay flow, user experience, and interaction mechanisms. |
| **Developers** | Develop the platform, core simulation features, data collection mechanisms, and supporting technical components. |
| **UI/UX Designer** | Design the user interface and improve the overall user experience. |
| **Testing Team / QA** | Support functional testing, identify issues, and validate the quality of the platform. |

### 5.2 Technology Resources

The project also required technical resources to support platform development and operation, including:

- Development tools and software.
- Infrastructure for hosting and operating the platform.
- Databases for storing user interactions and simulation data.
- Systems for collecting and managing simulation outcomes.
- Testing environments for validating the platform before deployment.

### 5.3 Domain and Testing Resources

Since ShrimpSim is a domain-specific simulation platform, collaboration with relevant stakeholders was an important project resource.

Key resources included:

- Shrimp farming knowledge and technical expertise.
- Input from lecturers and aquaculture specialists.
- Students and lecturers participating in Alpha testing.
- Selected shrimp farming households participating in Beta testing.
- Feedback collected during testing and validation activities.

### Resource Allocation Approach

The project was planned around cross-functional collaboration between technology specialists and aquaculture experts.

Domain experts were responsible for validating the accuracy and realism of farming processes and scenarios, while the technology team translated these requirements into a digital simulation platform.

The Business Analyst and Project Manager played a coordinating role to ensure that user needs, domain requirements, and technical implementation remained aligned throughout the project lifecycle.

## 6. Risk Management

The project team identified potential risks during the planning stage and evaluated them based on their **probability of occurrence** and **level of severity**.

The main risks identified in the project included:

- Data quality issues
- Technical issues within the game
- Data security
- Difficulty in recruiting suitable participants for testing
- Financial risks and unexpected costs
- Changes in stakeholder requirements
- Insufficient realism of the simulation
- Project schedule delays
- Ineffective Beta testing

### Risk Matrix

The project used a risk matrix to prioritize potential risks based on probability and severity.

| | Low Severity | High Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **Low Probability** | Ineffective Beta testing | Data security |
| **High Probability** | Technical issues in the game; Changes in requirements | Incomplete data; Budget overruns |

The matrix helped the project team identify risks that required closer monitoring and proactive management.

### Risk Register and Mitigation Plan

| Risk | Source / Stakeholder | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data Quality** | Internal and external – Project team, experts, and students | Insufficient preparation may lead to incomplete information for the project. Survey responses from students may also be inaccurate. | Develop a clear plan for each activity and regularly review progress and data to minimize errors. |
| **Technical Issues in the Game** | Internal – Game development team | The game may experience bugs, lag, or other technical issues. | Conduct thorough Alpha and Beta testing and maintain continuous communication within the development team. |
| **Data Security** | Internal and external – Development team and potential external attackers | User information may be exposed or leaked. | Apply strong encryption and conduct regular security checks. |
| **Lack of Suitable Testing Participants** | External – Shrimp farming households | Difficulty in finding suitable participants for testing. | Recruit participants through social media channels or through connections with the university. |
| **Financial Risk** | Internal – Project management | Actual costs may exceed the planned contingency budget. | Monitor costs closely, conduct careful planning, and regularly update cost estimates. |
| **Changes in Requirements** | External – University | The university may request additional changes or modifications to project requirements. | Maintain clear and effective communication with stakeholders. |
| **Simulation Realism** | Internal – Game development team | The simulation may not be realistic enough compared with actual shrimp farming practices. | Update and revise the simulation based on feedback. |
| **Schedule Delays** | Internal – Game development team | Development activities may take longer than originally planned. | Review and adjust the project plan when necessary. |




This approach allowed the project to remain flexible and incorporate feedback during development, particularly when refining gameplay scenarios and improving the user experience.
