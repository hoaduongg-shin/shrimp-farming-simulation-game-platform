# ShrimpSim – Shrimp Farming Simulation Game Platform

A data-driven shrimp farming simulation platform proposed as a digital transformation project for Vietnam National University of Agriculture (VNUA).

## Overview

ShrimpSim is an academic project that proposes a simulation-based platform to support practical learning in aquaculture.

The platform allows students to experience different stages and scenarios in shrimp farming through a virtual environment. In addition to supporting learning activities, the proposed platform is designed to generate user and simulation data that could support future analysis and development.

## Business Problem

The project identified limitations in traditional practical training, including dependence on physical farming environments and limited opportunities for students to experience different farming situations.

ShrimpSim was proposed as a digital solution to provide a more interactive and accessible simulation environment for aquaculture education.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution combines:

* A shrimp farming simulation game for practical learning.
* Simulation scenarios based on the shrimp farming process.
* Data collection from user interactions and decisions.
* A long-term data foundation that could support future analytics and AI applications.

## Project Highlights

This project covers the following areas:

* **Business Analysis** – analyzing the business context, problems, and project objectives.
* **Solution Design** – defining the proposed ShrimpSim solution and its value.
* **Project Planning** – developing the implementation approach, timeline, resources, and risk assessment.
* **Data Strategy** – proposing a data-driven approach and long-term data moat.
* **Financial Analysis** – estimating costs, projected revenue, cash flow, ROI, NPV, IRR, and payback period.
* **Performance Control** – proposing KPIs and evaluating the expected organizational impact of the project.
