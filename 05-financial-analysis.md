# 05. Financial Analysis

## 1. Financial Analysis Overview

The financial analysis was conducted to evaluate the economic feasibility of the proposed ShrimpSim project.

The analysis considered the project's initial investment, operating costs, expected financial benefits, and projected cash flows over a seven-year period.

Based on the project's financial assumptions, ShrimpSim was evaluated using the following investment indicators:

- Return on Investment (ROI)
- Net Present Value (NPV)
- Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
- Payback Period (PP)

The analysis aimed to determine whether the expected benefits of the project could justify the initial investment and long-term operating costs.

The overall financial evaluation was based on:

```text
Initial Investment
        +
Operating Costs
        ↓
Expected Financial Benefits
        ↓
Projected Cash Flows
        ↓
Investment Evaluation
        ↓
ROI | NPV | IRR | Payback Period
```

## 2. Initial Investment

The initial investment analysis was conducted to estimate the financial resources required to develop and implement the proposed ShrimpSim platform.

The investment covered the main resources required throughout the project, including human resources, technology and infrastructure, marketing and testing activities, office and travel expenses, and contingency costs.

### 2.1 Initial Investment Summary

Based on the project's financial model, the total initial investment was estimated at **1,969 million VND**.

The investment was allocated across the following major cost categories:

| Cost Category | Investment (Million VND) |
|---|---:|
| Human Resources | 1,656.5 |
| Tools and Infrastructure | 93.5 |
| Marketing and Testing | 30 |
| Office and Travel | 10 |
| Contingency | 179 |
| **Total Initial Investment** | **1,969** |

Human resources accounted for the largest proportion of the initial investment, reflecting the project's dependence on multiple roles involved in project management, business analysis, game development, design, technical development, and domain expertise.

A contingency budget was also included to provide additional financial flexibility for unexpected costs during project implementation.

## 5. Investment Evaluation

The financial feasibility of the proposed ShrimpSim project was evaluated using four key investment indicators: Return on Investment (ROI), Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and Payback Period.

### 5.1 Financial Performance Indicators

| Indicator | Result |
|---|---:|
| **Return on Investment (ROI)** | **114%** |
| **Net Present Value (NPV)** | **686 million VND** |
| **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)** | **16.9%** |
| **Payback Period** | **5.1 years** |

### 5.2 Interpretation of Results

The financial model produced a positive NPV of **686 million VND**, indicating that the projected financial benefits exceeded the estimated investment and operating costs under the assumptions used in the analysis.

The project achieved an estimated ROI of **114%**, while the IRR was calculated at **16.9%**.

The estimated payback period was approximately **5.1 years**, meaning that the project was projected to recover its initial investment after slightly more than five years of operation.

These results indicate that, based on the assumptions used in the financial model, ShrimpSim was considered financially feasible over the evaluation period.

> **Note:** These indicators represent projected results from the academic project's financial model and should not be interpreted as actual financial performance.

## 6. Financial Conclusion

The financial analysis evaluated the proposed ShrimpSim project based on its initial investment, projected revenue, expected cash flows, and key investment performance indicators.

Based on the assumptions used in the financial model, the project was estimated to achieve:

- A total initial investment of **1,969 million VND**.
- An ROI of **114%**.
- A positive NPV of **686 million VND**.
- An IRR of **16.9%**.
- A payback period of approximately **5.1 years**.

Overall, the financial analysis indicated that the proposed ShrimpSim project had the potential to generate positive financial returns over the seven-year evaluation period.

However, these results were based on projected revenue, cost assumptions, and the financial model developed by the project team. Therefore, the actual financial performance would depend on the implementation and future operation of the proposed platform.
